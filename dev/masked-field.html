      1. Type into the IBAN field and watch grouping appear.

      2. Backspace with the caret just after a space; the deletion widens over the space and removes the digit before
      it, not the space.

      3. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Z; the digit comes back (run in headed Safari — keyboard undo is inert headless).

      4. Paste FI2112345600000785, then FI21 1234 5600 0007 85; both present identically.

      5. Paste a 25-character string; nothing is truncated.

      6. Press "Swap blocks" with a value present; formatBlocks changes, the value regroups and nothing is
      lost.

      7. Press "Remove format"; both format properties are cleared, the field shows the raw unformatted value, readout's
      value is unchanged and formattedValue goes empty.

      8. Focus the IBAN field, put the caret mid-string, press its "Set value"; the caret stays put.

      9. Focus the unformatted field, put the caret mid-string, press its "Set value"; the caret jumps to the end,
      exactly as today.

      10. Switch to the Japanese IME and compose into the FI phone field; the candidate window is not disturbed.

      11. Turn on VoiceOver and repeat step 1; record what is announced. Nothing is announced for a rejected character,
      and nothing describes the shape — the prompt is aria-hidden.

      12. Type a space into the middle of a group in the IBAN field; the delimiter is consumed, the text does not change
      and the field does not flash as prevented.

      13. Type 9002011122 into the US phone field; it presents as +1 (900) 201-1122 and stops
      there — a mask truncates at its last slot, unlike the blocks.

      14. In the US phone field, put the caret right before a - and press Delete; the deletion widens over
      it and removes the digit on its far side.

      15. Type C10476001374 into the legacy mask field; it presents as
      C08000-10-47-600137-4 while value stays C10476001374.

      16. Press "Mask the IBAN field" with a value present; formatMask and formatBlocks are
      both set, one warning is logged and the mask wins — the groups are joined by - and the text stops at
      the last slot of the mask.

      17. Edit the IBAN field and Tab away; the change counter goes up by one per focus session, including for the
      keystrokes that the format applies itself.

      18. Look at the empty US phone, time, date, card and ZIP+4 fields; each shows the shape of its mask in the
      placeholder colour, and value and the input element are both empty.

      19. Type into the time field; the shape shrinks one character per keystroke and disappears once the mask is
      full.

      20. Type 12345 into the ZIP+4 field; the shape disappears. Type one more digit; the rest of the
      optional section appears as ___ and the - appears with the digit. Backspace; both
      disappear again.

      21. Type 1122 into the two-section field; the first section appears at the third digit and the second
      at the fifth, one at a time.

      22. Press "Toggle prompt" on the US phone field; the shape appears and disappears while the value stays put.

      23. Look at the "Phone with a prefix" field; the shape starts right after the +358 prefix, aligned
      with the caret.

      24. Look at the "Time with a placeholder" field while it is empty; the placeholder shows and the shape does not.
      Type one digit; the placeholder goes and the shape appears.

      25. Type 123 into the time field and Tab away; it turns invalid and shows its error message. Type the
      fourth digit and Tab away; it turns valid. The same for the date field at four of eight digits.

      26. Clear the time field and Tab away; it is valid again — an empty value is left to required.

      27. Look at the readouts of the card and time fields; both report inputMode: "numeric", which no
      attribute on the card field asks for — an all-digit mask derives it. The IBAN and legacy readouts report
      "", and the US and FI phone readouts report the tel the page set.

      28. Type ٣٤٥٦ into the unicode field, or paste it; the field shows 3456 and
      value is 3456. Press "Set ٣٤٥٦"; the field shows 3456,
      value stays ٣٤٥٦ and one warning is logged.

      29. Look at the readonly and disabled fields; both show a value with its shape and both report
      checkValidity(): true, even though the value does not fill the mask.

      30. Set formatPrompt on the IBAN field from the console; no shape appears, since blocks grow with the
      value and have no fixed shape to show.
